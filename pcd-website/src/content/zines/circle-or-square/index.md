**Circle or square?** is an eight-panel activity guide for people new to p5.js.
Participants open the [p5.js editor](https://editor.p5js.org), create a 600×600 canvas, 
and use nested `for` loops to walk a 12×12 grid. In each cell, `random()` and `if`/`else` 
choose the shape: a yellow circle about 60% of the time, or a green pair of nested 
squares about 40% of the time. Stroke color and stroke weight also vary at random.
The last panel prints the full sketch and three prompts: change the probability, change 
the grid spacing, and add new shapes.
The same zine is available in Turkish (*Çember mi, kare mi?*), English, and French 
(*Cercle ou carré ?*)
