Learn p5.js by coding and assembling your own 8-page zine! CTRRRL-ZINE takes you through a short history of zines and the cut-and-paste mentality, and access to the source code used to create the layout. Scan the QR code on the back of the zine or visit https://openprocessing.org/@ctrrrlzines/2145820 to access the step-by-step tutorial. Modify this template to code, print, and distribute your own zines and posters.
