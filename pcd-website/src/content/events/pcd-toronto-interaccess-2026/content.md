Processing Community Day at InterAccess is a show-and-tell for people who use Processing, the creative coding platform. Come see what others have built, hear how they built it, and share your own work if you wish.
The event is free and open to everyone, whether you are new to creative coding or have worked with Processing for years. Expect short presentations, demos, and conversation with a community of artists, educators, and developers.
