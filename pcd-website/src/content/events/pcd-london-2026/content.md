Join a full day of celebrating creative computing at Peckham Digital's yearly festival!

At Peckham Digital our main mission is to expand access to creative computing, as such we have collaborated with Considerate Digital, Creative Computing Institute, Goldsmiths, SpokeBoard, and UCL Art + Tech to deliver five workshops for 18-25 year old who are not in education or employment.

Fear not, if you do not fit in this category as there are plenty other events you can join:

- there will be a dedicated p5.js and Processing display in the exhibition space at The Hub
- Curator Melanie Lenz will be talking abut Vera Molnar 
- We are going to host a townhall focused on the Learning to Teach 
- The night will continue with live audio-visual performances

Please do join us in this celebration! 

This event was made possible with the support from the Generative Art Foundation and all our collaborators.
