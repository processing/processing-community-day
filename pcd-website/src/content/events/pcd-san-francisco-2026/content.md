**Gray Area** and **TIAT** are teaming up for Processing Community Day San Francisco.

On October 3, 2026, the Grand Theater will come alive with an afternoon of talks and workshops from 1 – 6 PM.

We’re celebrating 25 years of [Processing](https://processing.org/), the creative coding platform that has shaped generations of artists, designers, educators, and programmers around the world.

Come discover what the community is building today with Processing and p5.js, and where creative coding is heading next.

## Workshops

* [Introduction to libprocessing](https://luma.com/lzicsusw) with Charlotte McElwain, 1–3pm
* [Working with Variable Type in p5.js](https://luma.com/cr46cjp7) with Ivan Zhao, 1–3pm

## Talks
Join us 4–6pm for [lightning talks](https://luma.com/v6dftzxi) on community projects with Processing and p5.js!
