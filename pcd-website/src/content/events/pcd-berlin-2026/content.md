Processing Community Day is a free event to celebrate the intersection of design, art, and code. It’s a unique chance for everyone working in any of those fields, including those not normally represented in the fields of engineering and technology, to meet in person and share their passion!

​We will be meeting at the Media University of Applied Sciences, in the "Media Lounge". More info TBD.​

​The event will consist of a show case of processing or creative coding sketches (we would love to see your work!), with a collective coding session (without AI this time?) and for the party afterwards we would love to see what you coded vibing to some music!
