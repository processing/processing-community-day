Processing Community Day @ PDX (PCD @ PDX) is a free community gathering for people interested in art, design, technology, education, and creative coding.

Join us for hands-on demonstrations, project sharing, and conversations about the possibilities of art and technology.

The afternoon will include opportunities to:

Experiment with creative coding and interactive technologies
See projects and demonstrations from local community members
Talk with others working across art, design, education, and technology
Share something you are working on
Learn more about Processing, p5.js, physical computing, and related creative practices
No previous coding experience is required. Whether you are completely new to creative coding or already make work with tools like Processing and p5.js, you are welcome.
