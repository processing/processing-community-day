Processing Community Day[s] Tehran is a week-long gathering celebrating creative coding, computational art, and open-source culture. The program launches with opening-day keynote presentations by Zahra Zargari, Mohammadreza Nemati, and Ali Motamedi, followed throughout the week by hands-on workshops facilitated by Pedram Sadegh-Beyki, Zahra Zargari, and Narges Mostafalou. Alongside these sessions, a curated code-art exhibition runs continuously in the museum space, showcasing generative and software-based artworks from invited local and international artists.
